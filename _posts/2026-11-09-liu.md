---
title: "Hamilton-Jacobi Equations on Lie Groups: Hopf Formulas, Peter-Weyl Dynamic Programming, and High-Dimensional Computation"
speaker:
  name: Shanqing Liu
  affil: Brown, Applied Math
  url: https://shanqingliu.github.io/
---

Many optimal control problems involving orientations and translations are
naturally posed on Lie groups, including applications in robotics, rigid-body
motion, vehicle dynamics, and multi-body systems. Their Hamilton–Jacobi
equations have the usual difficulties of nonlinearity, non-smoothness, and
curse-of-dimensionality, and also with geometric difficulties such as
noncommutativity and the lack of global coordinates. We present two
structure-preserving approaches. The first is a global Hopf-type formula for
left-invariant problems, reducing the value function to a finite-dimensional
minimization over the initial covector and yielding a grid-free solver with
cost linear in the group dimension. The second is a Peter–Weyl dynamic
programming scheme on compact groups, for more general problems, where a
positive finite-rank Fejér-type filter restores monotonicity and gives a
chart-free method with uniform convergence for Lipschitz data. We conclude
with ongoing work on high-dimensional products of groups, including (classical
and tropical) tensor methods.
